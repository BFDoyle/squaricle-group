training solutions that create great people

We are focused on helping human to human connections.

We are a values driven business, with an absolute focus on improving people performance.

Squaricle Group is made up of several great brands, delivering training that makes truly great people.

The Group was founded in 2020 as ted Learning Group and changed its name in June 2021 to reflect the diversity of our brands and partners. Several of our brands have been trading for many years and have built up strong reputations for the services, products and solutions they provide. We created the Group as a way of combining great brands and creating a partnership route for other niche L&D offers.

We work in partnership with our clients and want to feel like an extension of your own L&D team. We spend time getting to know your business so that any product or service we provide feels completely relevant to your people.

Whatever your development needs, we have a solution for you. All of our offers are multi-channel and can be delivered via the classroom, virtual classroom or online learning.

Our brands are ted Learning, ted HuB, emerging-u & CurioTraining

ted Learning

ted Learning delivers both classroom-based, virtual and online learning using theatre-based learning. We use professional actors and experienced trainers and deliver any form of training that involves human interaction.

Find out more

ted HuB

The ted Learning HuB is a vibrant online learning community where our clients’ learners access their digital and virtual training, including pre and post-course work. Learners also get life-long access to free ted Express courses, podcasts, videos and the latest insights.

Visit the ted HuB

emerging-u

emerging-u is a Leadership Learning and Development Agency that specialises in culture change, leadership development and team effectiveness. We create the conditions for change.

Find out more

CurioTraining

CurioTraining is our online workplace training brand. Practical, accredited courses for individuals, teams and organisations that get people ready for real work. Learn online on any device, with certificates included and easy team enrolment for managers.

Visit curiotraining.com

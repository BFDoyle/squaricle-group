People Principles

We know that our people are our biggest and most important asset, and the key to our success.

That’s why we work hard to create a fun, collaborative, inclusive culture and environment where our people thrive, enabling individual and organisational success.

Our People Strategy is based on three fundamentals that form the context for everything relating to the Squaricle Group team:

Discover

the right people for the right roles in our business.

Nurture

their talent, creating sustainable value and driving growth.

Retain

them in the business to ensure our ongoing and long-term success.

All our people related policies, processes, resources and ways of working support the delivery of these fundamentals, and we’re always looking at how to improve and develop our people focus further.

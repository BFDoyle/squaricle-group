Why join our family?

Squaricle Group is all about our people. We want a team of talented and empowered people that can help us deliver our vision.

Our team are passionate about working at Squaricle Group. We love bringing in new talent, to challenge our ways of working, bring new skills and perspectives and add real value to our organisation.

The benefits of working at the Squaricle Group

To do this we want to attract, engage and develop a team of diverse, creative and empowered individuals that reflects the whole UK, our clients and our learners.

We recognise that we currently don’t represent our Black and Asian communities and so we’d particularly welcome applications from people from these groups. We’re based in a variety of different locations across the country, yet we work exceptionally well together, so wherever you’re based in the UK, we’d love to hear from you.

As well as being part of a creative and innovative team, we are also pleased to offer all of these employee benefits if you join our team.

Flexible working hours

25 days starting holiday (FT)

Social events

Contributory pension

Cycle to work scheme

Fun working environment
